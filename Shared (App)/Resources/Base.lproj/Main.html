Viewer

A simple companion app for your Safari extension.

You can turn on Viewer’s Safari extension in Settings.

You can turn on Viewer’s extension in Safari Extensions preferences.

Viewer’s extension is currently on. You can turn it off in Safari Extensions preferences.

Viewer’s extension is currently off. You can turn it on in Safari Extensions preferences.

What Viewer does

  • Installs a Safari web extension powered by modern WebExtensions APIs.
  • Keeps the native app and extension in sync across iOS and macOS.
  • Gives you a quick way to open Safari’s extension settings when needed.
